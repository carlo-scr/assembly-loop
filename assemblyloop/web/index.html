    Nudging

    Forward is away from the robot's base. Up and down follow the gripper's own axis, so it should point at the table. Turning spins the gripper in place (only its last joint moves). Every nudge is slow and ends by itself if something is in the way.

    Tilting the gripper down keeps the flange in place and swings the fingertips sideways: keep the space around the gripper free.

    Showing it the places

    Stand a block where it should be picked up, nudge the open gripper around it (fingertips a few millimetres above the table) and press "Save spot here". Do the same for every other place, then press Done. A spot remembers how the gripper was turned there. The tilt of the gripper must be the same at every spot.

    The lift is how far above the highest spot the arm travels between spots.

    Find the table by itself

    Instead of teaching spots by hand. Bring the gripper over the middle of the area the blocks will lie in, pointing straight down. Hold a bar or plank between the open jaws by its ends, lying level, the jaws across its short side, and press Close. Then press Find the table and let the arm move: the gripper comes straight down slowly until the bar meets the mat, lets go, and the label reads ARMED. Stop it if it visibly pushes into the table. The tower will stand where the bar was put down.

    "The gripper is at the table now": you have brought the fingertips down onto the mat yourself; nothing comes down, the bar is let go there. "Put the area here": the table was found before and only the place should change; bring the gripper over the new middle at any height and it comes straight down to the height found before.

    A tower

    Stand one block on every spot except the one the tower goes on, choose that spot, enter the block height and press Build. The first block goes down at the taught height and each next one a block height higher, let go a little above the block below. Take down returns every block to its own spot, top first.

    Start with both boxes ticked: "rehearse in the air" does the whole travel without coming down, and "one step at a time" waits for Next step before every step.

    Show the camera the table

    Once, and again whenever the camera or the table was moved. Lay ONE bar or plank flat on a spot, jaws across its short side, say under "Stands on" (Blocks) which spot that is, and keep everything else off the mat. The arm first tries a few places to wait and keeps the one from which the camera sees most of the mat, then carries the block to places around where it lies (only where it reaches and the mat is) and steps aside for a picture after each. Blocks must lie on the grey mat and must not touch each other.
