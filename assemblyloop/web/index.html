    Moving the gripper

    Hold the grip while giving a command. W / S move away from and towards the base, A / D move left and right, and Q / E move up and down along the gripper’s axis. Z / C turn the gripper; F / R close and open it.

    Each press moves by the selected increment. Start with 1 mm near the table. In a stand-in session, hold the space bar instead of the grip.

    Teaching spots

    Stand a block at a pickup or placement point. Nudge the open gripper around it, with the fingertips a few millimetres above the table, then save the spot. Repeat for the other spots and choose Finish teaching.

    A spot remembers the gripper's rotation. Keep the same tilt at every spot. Travel lift is the clearance above the highest spot when moving between them.

    Find the table

    Point the gripper straight down above the centre of the work area. Hold a green or blue bar level between the open jaws, with the jaws across its short side, and close the gripper.

    Find the table lowers the bar slowly until it meets the mat, releases it, and creates the spots. Hold the grip throughout; release it if the arm visibly pushes into the table. The tower spot is where the bar lands.

    If the fingertips are already on the mat, choose Gripper is at the table: the bar is released there without lowering.

    Building a tower

    Stand one block on every spot except the tower spot. Choose that spot, enter the block height, and choose Build tower. The first block goes to the taught height; each next block goes one block height higher and is released just above the one below.

    Take down returns each block to its original spot, top first. Build & take down repeats this for the chosen number of rounds.

    Start with Rehearse in the air and One step at a time enabled. Rehearsal follows the travel route without coming down to the blocks.

    Calibrating the camera

    Teach spots at opposite corners of the work area. Lay one bar or slab flat on a taught spot with the jaws across its short side. Select the block and its source spot in Camera calibration.

    Keep everything else off the mat. Hold the grip while the arm carries the block to each sample position and steps aside for a picture. Repeat calibration whenever the camera or table moves. For detection, blocks must lie apart on the grey mat.
