    Up is away from the robot. Blocks drop straight down until they meet something, as when the arm puts them down.

    Does it stand?

    After every step the program works out whether what stands so far can carry its own weight: wood on wood, nothing glued, only faces that touch push. The bars show how far the table could be tilted before something tips over; higher is safer. Below 4° a small bump, such as the gripper letting go, may topple it.

    It also checks what the arm needs: every block comes straight down from above onto what is already there, the jaws close across its short side, and the fingers need room on both sides when they open. The gripper's sizes are UFACTORY's, not measured on the rig.
